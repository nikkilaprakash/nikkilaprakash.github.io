About

I bridge engineering and AI, taking ideas from prototype to production systems that run millions of times a day at low latency.

At PayPal I developed a real-time consent platform serving PayPal, Venmo and Honey across three regions, built a low-latency decisioning service handling 10M requests a day, and shipped LLM tools using the OpenAI API, RAG and the Model Context Protocol that turned slow, policy-heavy work into self-service. One of those ideas became a filed patent for an autonomous multi-agent system that detects and resolves production errors.

I'm now doing an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Edinburgh, focused on machine learning, deep learning, distributed systems and deploying ML in production. I'm part of CompSoc, QuantSoc and EdinburghAI, and play badminton for the university club. Away from the keyboard, I've mentored new engineers and have spent years volunteering, at PayPal's Tech for Good programme and running campaigns with the National Service Scheme.
